## 4. Important notes

- I recommend keeping `torch` and `torchvision` in a separate installation step, rather than inside the requirements file.
- Don't add `gradio`, `gradio-client`, or `diffusers` unless your project needs them.
- Let pip resolve supporting packages such as Accelerate and Datasets rather than guessing their versions.
- Your Transformers 4.57.6 installation requires a compatible Hub version. See the [Transformers installation documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/v4.57.0/installation). <Cite refs={["turn391913search0","turn391913search3"]}/>

**One final recommendation:** Keep this requirements file in your project folder and use it whenever you recreate the environment.

This will make your Colab LLM fine-tuning setup much easier to reproduce.
